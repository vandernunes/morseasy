# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Amateur radio runs on people being patient with beginners. Somebody slowed down
their fist so you could make your first contact. Act like that person here.

## Expected

- Be patient with people who are new. This project exists for them.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming someone is wrong.
- Correct errors about the mode directly and kindly — accuracy matters, tone
  costs nothing.
- Accept that people learn at different speeds, on different rigs, with
  different amounts of time.

## Not acceptable

- Gatekeeping. "Real hams learned it the hard way" is not feedback.
- Harassment, personal attacks, or comments about anyone's identity.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Deliberately making a beginner feel stupid for asking.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this
project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/vandernunes/morseasy/security/advisories/new)
or by direct message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses range from a warning to a permanent ban, at the maintainer's
discretion. Being technically right does not exempt anyone.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
kept short so it actually gets read.
